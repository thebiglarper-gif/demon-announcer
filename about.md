# Demon Announcer

Every time you beat a demon for the first time, the Demon Announcer Discord bot posts it in your server.

1. In Discord, type **/link**. The bot gives you a secret key.
2. In GD, open this mod's settings. Paste the key into **Bot Key** and the bot's address into **Bot Address**.
3. Go beat a demon!

Practice mode and start-position runs are not announced.
